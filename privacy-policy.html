FieldScope Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2026

FieldScope is a field-research tool for mapping downtown Charleston neighborhoods. This policy describes what data the app collects, how it is used, and where it is stored. By using FieldScope you agree to the practices described below.

What we collect

Location data. When you start a walk session, FieldScope records your GPS coordinates to draw your walking route, geotag photos and notes, and determine which neighborhood you are in. Location is sampled while the app is open and continues in the background during an active walk session so the full route is captured. You can stop a session at any time, and you can revoke location permission at any time from iOS Settings.

Photos. Photos you capture inside FieldScope are stored on your device. Each photo is tagged with the GPS coordinate, timestamp, neighborhood, an optional written note, and a category you select. Photos already in your photo library are not accessed unless you explicitly choose them.

Field-research content. Written notes, micro-survey responses (noise, comfort, sightlines, ground-floor use, tree canopy), sightline records (heading and visible targets), quick-tag pins, and a checklist of landmarks you have visited.

FieldScope does not collect your name, email address, contacts, advertising identifiers, device identifiers, browsing history, or any other personal data beyond what is described above.

Where your data is stored

On your device. All field-research content is stored locally on your iPhone or iPad using Apple's SwiftData framework. This local copy is the primary store and works fully offline.

Cloud sync via Supabase. When your device has network connectivity, FieldScope syncs your walk sessions, track points, photos, notes, survey responses, sightline observations, quick tags, and checklist state to a private Supabase project hosted on Amazon Web Services in the United States. Photo image files are uploaded to Supabase Storage. The purpose of cloud sync is backup and the ability to view your research from other devices or share it with collaborators.

Who can see your data

  • You, on any device that signs in to your FieldScope account.
  • People you explicitly share research with.
  • Supabase, Inc., as our infrastructure provider, under their privacy policy. Supabase processes data on our behalf and does not use it for any other purpose.

We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics vendors. FieldScope contains no third-party advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics SDKs, and no tracking technologies.

How long we keep your data

Data stays on your device until you delete it from inside FieldScope or uninstall the app. Data synced to Supabase is retained until you delete it from the app, which removes it from both the device and the cloud copy on the next sync. You can request full deletion of any cloud-stored data by contacting us at the address below.

Children

FieldScope is intended for use by adults conducting field research. It is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your rights

You can:

  • Revoke camera or location permission at any time from iOS Settings → Privacy & Security.
  • Delete individual photos, notes, sessions, or other research items from inside the app.
  • Uninstall FieldScope to remove all on-device data.
  • Request deletion of cloud-stored data by emailing the contact address below.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a material way, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, surface a notice inside the app before the change takes effect.
